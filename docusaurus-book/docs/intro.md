# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics textbook. This comprehensive guide covers the fundamental concepts, development techniques, and practical applications of physical artificial intelligence and humanoid robotics.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the fundamental principles of Physical AI
- Learn about embodied intelligence and its applications
- Explore the relationship between perception, action, and cognition in robotic systems
- Gain familiarity with key technologies and platforms in humanoid robotics

## What You'll Learn

This textbook is organized into several key modules:

1. **Introduction to Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence**: Foundational concepts and principles
2. **ROS 2 Fundamentals**: Robot Operating System for humanoid robotics applications
3. **Robot Simulation**: Using Gazebo and Unity for simulation environments
4. **NVIDIA Isaac Platform**: AI hardware and software for robotics applications
5. **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Systems**: Integration of perception, language, and action
6. **Conversational Robotics**: Natural human-robot interaction

## Summary

This introduction chapter provides the foundation for understanding how physical AI differs from traditional AI approaches by emphasizing the importance of embodiment, interaction with the physical world, and the integration of perception, cognition, and action. As you progress through this textbook, you'll gain both theoretical knowledge and practical skills needed to develop humanoid robotic systems.
