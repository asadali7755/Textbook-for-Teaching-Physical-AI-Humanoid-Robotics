# Week 5 - Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Systems

This week focuses on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems, which integrate perception, language understanding, and motor control for advanced humanoid robotics.

## Topics to Cover

- Architecture and principles of VLA systems
- Integration of vision, language, and action modalities
- Learning paradigms for VLA systems
- Implementation for humanoid robotics applications
- Challenges and limitations of VLA systems

## Reading Assignment

- Vision-Language-Action systems chapter (index.md)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the architecture and principles of Vision-Language-Action systems
- Implement VLA models for humanoid robotics applications
- Apply VLA systems to real-world manipulation and navigation tasks
- Evaluate the strengths and limitations of VLA approaches
- Design VLA-based solutions for robotics challenges

## Practical Exercises

- Explore VLA model architectures and implementations
- Review research papers on recent VLA developments
- Analyze how VLA systems integrate with humanoid robot platforms
- Compare VLA approaches with traditional modular robotics

## Estimated Hours

- Reading: 3 hours
- Research and exploration: 6 hours
- Review and analysis: 3 hours
