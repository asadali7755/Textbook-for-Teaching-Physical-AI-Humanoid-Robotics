# Week 1 - Introduction to Physical AI

This week introduces the foundational concepts of Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence that will underpin the rest of the textbook.

## Topics to Cover

- Definition and scope of Physical AI
- Distinction from traditional AI approaches
- The role of embodiment in intelligence
- Overview of the humanoid robotics field
- Course objectives and structure

## Reading Assignment

- Introduction chapter (this document)
- Additional resources on the history of AI and robotics

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Define Physical AI and explain its significance
- Describe the concept of embodied intelligence
- Understand the basic principles that differentiate physical AI from traditional AI
- Identify key challenges in humanoid robotics

## Estimated Hours

- Reading: 3 hours
- Reflection and note-taking: 2 hours
- Exploring additional resources: 2 hours
